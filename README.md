# One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off

One line about office life:

> The company preaches transparency, so the meeting rooms are glass and the salary bands are frosted.

## Why it works

The joke takes the company's own sermon — transparency — and holds it up against the two things it actually shows: meeting rooms walled in glass, salary bands frosted over. The contrast is the whole joke: the glass is real, the frost is real, and the only thing transparent is the hypocrisy. One read, no setup to explain, and the punchline lands on the last word.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (and `./entry.txt` for local verification).
